My Personal Development

Personal development is about developing my decisions, habits and preparation towards the future. I feel that money management could be an area that is important in personal development.

George S. Clason's The Richest Man in Babylon is a book that teaches principles about money by using stories in Babylon from long ago. The book is about saving, spending and the development of wealth.

I will look at two chapters from the book. The chapters are Seven Cures for a Lean Purse and The Five Laws of Gold. I feel that these two chapters can relate to my situation as a student.

Jump to My Biggest Lesson

Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

In this chapter Arkad teaches seven ways a person can improve their money. I found that it is not only about earning money but also how you use the money that you receive.

1. Start Thy Purse to Fattening

The first cure is about the person holding on to one in ten of their earnings rather than spending all that they receive.

I could relate this to me as a student by holding on to a percentage of the money I receive before I go out and purchase other items that I need. I feel that regular saving should be preferred over only saving when extra money is available.

2. Control Thy Expenditures

This cure deals with separating necessary items from non necessary items. A person can easily spend money without knowing how much they have spent when there is not a budget set.

I can apply this to myself as a student by budgeting money for transportation, food and other necessary needs and not spending on non necessary items such as entertainment.

3. Make Thy Gold Multiply

This cure talks about the ability to make your money make more money by putting your money in an investment that is suited to it. This is to make your money work for you rather than just putting it away with no purpose.

I can apply this to myself by learning about different types of investments and how they can work to increase a person's money. After I have learnt how and what investments work I can use my money in an investment that will work to increase my money.

4. Guard Thy Treasures from Loss

This cure is about protecting one's money from bad decisions. Arkad advises one to think about the safety of one's money before focusing on making money.

I could apply this to myself by looking into financial and business opportunities and asking for advice from people who have experience in such things before using my money.

5. Make of Thy Dwelling a Profitable Investment

This cure is about finding a safe and stable home and also spending money on a home in a safe way.

I could apply this when I am looking at owning property in the future. As a current student I could also apply this to my current place of living and rent.

6. Insure a Future Income

This cure is about preparing for the future and also preparing in case one is not able to work in the future.

I could use this to help me in my present day also. I should plan for my future and save money for the unexpected as well as for the future.

7. Increase Thy Ability to Earn

This cure is about the ability to increase your ability to earn more money by increasing your knowledge and skills.

This one is important to me as a Computing student. By increasing my skills in software development and web development I will be increasing my ability to make more money in the future.

The Five Laws of Gold

In this chapter Arkad has the opportunity to test his son Nomasir to see if he can handle money wisely. Nomasir makes mistakes with money and later learns the Five Laws of Gold.

This can relate to me as a student in that someone can have money but not know how to use it wisely. Financial knowledge and good judgement are also important.

First Law of Gold

Gold is more available to the person who saves one in ten of their money to the future.

I can apply this by treating saving money as a responsibility rather than an optional matter.

Second Law of Gold

Gold works for the person who uses it in an investment in which it can make more money.

I can apply this to myself by learning about how money can make more money by using it in wise investments.

Third Law of Gold

Gold is safe when it is used in accordance with the advice of those who have experience in money matters.

I believe this is important because advice from someone who has experience can help me avoid some mistakes.

Fourth Law of Gold

Gold can be lost by investing in opportunities and businesses that you do not fully understand.

I can apply this to myself by researching a business opportunity and making a decision after researching rather than making a decision based on what others are doing.

Fifth Law of Gold

Gold can be lost by going after unattainable profits or by following advice from inexperienced people.

I can apply this to myself by not going after get-rich-quick schemes and focusing on realistic financial goals.

Application of the Lessons to My Life

I believe the lessons from these two chapters apply to me as a student as I am still learning about managing and saving money.

One lesson I can apply to myself is to budget money and then hold on to a certain percentage that can be used for savings before buying non necessary items.

I can also apply lessons about investing in my education. By improving technical, communication and programming skills I can increase my ability to earn more money in the future.

I have also learned from this book to be more aware of financial risks. I should not rush into an investment just because someone has promised that I can make large sums of money.

My Biggest Lesson

My biggest lesson from The Richest Man in Babylon is that managing money is more important than earning a large sum of money.

The lessons that I have learnt teach me that a person can start building wealth with whatever income they earn so long as they can save and spend wisely.

I have also learnt that earning money and managing money are not one and the same. Someone can earn a lot of money but still have money problems if they do not know how to spend money wisely.

I would like to continue improving my ability to manage money, save and make wise financial decisions. I believe that will help me become more independent and responsible with my money in the future.

Reference

Clason, G. S. (1988). The richest man in Babylon. Signet.
