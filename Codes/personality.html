About My Personality

I believe my personality plays an important role in how I approach school, challenges, and everyday life. Three traits that describe me best are determination, curiosity, and competitiveness.

My Three Strongest Personality Traits

1. Determination

I would describe myself as a determined student. I aim to persevere when in a difficult situation. In my course of study I am currently studying Computing and there are many difficult programming assignments and tasks.

I use determination to keep trying to find answers and keep practising rather than failing to try. I aim to gain an interest and confidence in my own studies.

2. Curiosity

I have always had an interest in technology and in seeing how things work. I also have an interest in computers, programming and new technology.

Curiosity allows me to gain more knowledge in my studies as I look to find out more than that which is given to me in the course. I also aim to ask questions and try alternative solutions to any issue.

3. Competitiveness

I am quite competitive especially in video games. I like to test my own skills and aim to get better at my particular game by practicing.

This quality also translates into my studies. I like to set myself targets and aim to achieve them. I do however recognise that excessive competitiveness can cause stress. I aim to focus on my own progress rather than comparing myself to others.

Using AI as a Tool, Not a Crutch

My Thoughts on Artificial Intelligence

"LLMs such as Copilot, Claude and ChatGPT should be used as a tool rather than a crutch" essentially suggests that AI can aid the learning process but should not replace the student in all thinking.

As a Computing student AI can play a role in the learning of computer science. If I am confused about a problem in Java or C++ I can use AI to explain the problem, give me code examples or to assist in explaining why my code may not be functioning as intended.

Using AI in learning is different from being completely reliant on AI. A student who uses AI to complete an entire assignment without any understanding of the material presented has the potential to fail on practical or exam tasks as AI will not be available in these circumstances.

Furthermore AI can also produce incorrect solutions. Students should always refer to course material, textbooks, documentation and lecture slides as reliable sources of knowledge and check any information provided by AI. AI should not be used to avoid research or critical thinking.

Personally I feel the most appropriate use of AI would be to try to solve a problem yourself before turning to AI for feedback, extra practice, clarification or even attempting the problem initially. This ensures a student is still able to build their own skills and problem solving whilst still making use of technology.

I feel that AI can play a part in effective learning provided it is used appropriately. The aim should be to be a more effective student rather than just completing tasks at a faster rate.
